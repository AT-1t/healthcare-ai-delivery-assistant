SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION NOTES — NOT A SCIENTIFIC SOURCE OR CLINICAL GUIDANCE.
EGFR is a receptor tyrosine kinase studied in non-small-cell lung cancer research.
Some research programs stratify experimental analyses by EGFR variants, including L858R and exon 19 deletions.
Binding affinity prediction models may use molecular fingerprints and curated interaction datasets.
Model evaluation should separate training and test data and examine potential data leakage.
A high R-squared score on a held-out dataset does not establish clinical effectiveness.
Research assistants should cite primary publications and communicate limitations.
