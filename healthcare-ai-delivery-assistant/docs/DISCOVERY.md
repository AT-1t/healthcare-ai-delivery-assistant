# Hypothetical Client Discovery Plan
**This is a simulated engagement; no client interviews have occurred.**

## Discovery workshop (60 minutes)
1. Map current literature-review workflow and time per question (15 min).
2. Identify high-frequency questions and source types (15 min).
3. Rank pain points by volume, cost, and risk (10 min).
4. Review privacy, licensing, and evidence standards (10 min).
5. Agree MVP boundaries, baseline metrics, and next steps (10 min).

## Interview questions
- What counts as a trustworthy citation in your workflow?
- How do you verify a claim today?
- Which documents are public versus restricted?
- What errors would make the assistant unusable?
- Who approves an answer before downstream use?

## Hypotheses to validate
H1: Cited passage retrieval reduces time-to-evidence by at least 25%.
H2: Users prefer explicit abstention over unsupported synthesis.
H3: Public-document-only scope is sufficient for an initial pilot.

## Research plan
Observe 5–8 consenting research staff; baseline 10 tasks per participant; run a crossover comparison with and without assistant; measure completion time, citation correctness, and user trust. All counts are proposed, not performed.
