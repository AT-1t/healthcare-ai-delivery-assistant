# Illustrative AI ROI Business Case (Assumptions, Not Observed Results)

Assume 12 researchers, 20 literature questions/person/week, baseline 12 minutes/question, 25% time reduction, loaded labor cost $65/hour, 48 working weeks/year.

- Questions/year = 12 × 20 × 48 = **11,520**
- Hours saved/year = 11,520 × (12/60) × 25% = **576**
- Gross capacity value = 576 × $65 = **$37,440/year**
- Hypothetical annual API/hosting/support costs = **$9,000**
- Hypothetical one-time implementation = **$12,000**
- First-year net benefit = $37,440 − $9,000 − $12,000 = **$16,440**
- First-year ROI = $16,440 / ($9,000 + $12,000) = **78.3%**

**Important:** These are illustrative assumptions, not verified customer savings or actual model costs. Saved time is capacity value, not automatically cash savings. Excludes quality-review time, security/compliance, change management, and error costs. Validate through an actual pilot before using for investment decisions.

## Measurement plan
Instrument task start/end, collect opt-in baseline and assisted task times, sample citation correctness, measure API tokens/cost, and report outcomes with confidence intervals. Do not store patient information.
